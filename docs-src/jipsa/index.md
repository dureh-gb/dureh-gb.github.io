# 집사 Jipsa

A butler for your machines. Jipsa turns your computers on and off through the smart-home plugs you already use.

*Jipsa* (집사) is the steward who keeps a household running.

## What it does

- Runs as a small service on one of your Linux computers, with a command line and a local web page.
- Switches Alexa-connected plugs on and off by name, and reports whether the change was confirmed.
- Keeps its sign-in encrypted on that computer. Your password is never stored.
- Starts a work computer in the morning so the rest of the family of tools can get going.

## Status

**Closed beta.** Jipsa runs for the workshop's own computers. There is no public release yet.

## Where it fits

Jipsa is the power step. It switches a computer on before the other tools need it, and off again when the day is done. Once the computer is up, [Dureh](../dureh/index.md) reaches it over SSH, and [Pumasi](../pumasi/index.md) shows its screen.

- Runs on: Linux, with Amazon Alexa plugs.
