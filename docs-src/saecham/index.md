# 새참 Saecham

A small terminal-session keeper for Windows, in the spirit of tmux. Close the window, and your session is still there when you come back.

*Saecham* (새참) is the snack brought out between field work: a short break, and the work carries on.

## What it is

Saecham keeps terminal sessions alive on Windows. A session survives a closed window, so you can step away and pick up where you left off. It is the tmux-like part of the Windows desk.

## Status

**Planned.** Nothing is released yet. Saecham is planned together with the Dureh desktop for Windows, which is itself in progress.

## Where it fits

- [Dureh](../dureh/index.md) is the desk: terminal, files, port forwards, screens and agents in one window. Saecham will keep the Windows terminal sessions behind that desk alive.
- Runs on: Windows (planned).
